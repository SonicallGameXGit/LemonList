title: Icon Kit
date: 2026-10-11
---

Your profile picture is now your Geometry Dash icon. The new garage in the dashboard settings works just like the one in the game.

- Pick an icon for every mode: cube, ship, ball, UFO, wave, robot, spider, swing and jetpack
- All 107 colors of the game for the primary, secondary and glow colors
- The last mode you picked becomes your avatar, like on the game leaderboards
- Profiles show your whole icon kit
