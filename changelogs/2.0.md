---
title: Records and admin tools
date: 2026-10-11
---

The list became fully interactive: beat a level, send your record and climb the leaderboard.

- **Submit records** with a completion video and follow their status on your dashboard
- **Rate how engaging** a level was, once you beat it. The engagement of a level is built from all ratings
- Upload your own avatar
- Admins got their own panel: review records, verify accounts against impersonation, manage roles and bans, add and edit levels
- Admins reorder the Main List by dragging levels, every move shows up in the list changes
