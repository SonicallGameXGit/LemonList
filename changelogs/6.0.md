---
title: Mobile support
date: 2026-10-23
---

LemonList now fits your phone. The pages were reworked for small screens, so you can check the list, your records and the leaderboard on the go.

- Level cells stack on phones: the preview first, then the name and the stats, and long names wrap instead of getting cut off
- The difficulty filter of the Main List shows one row of faces, press **Show more** to see all of them
- Search boxes got a **Search** button, which also closes the keyboard on phones
- Beaten levels on player profiles show their previews on phones
- The stats of a level page fit in one row under its preview
