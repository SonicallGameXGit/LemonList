---
title: Level submissions
date: 2026-10-12
---

Beat a demon that isn't on the list yet? Send it to us, and once the admins accept it the level joins the list with your record on it.

- **Submit levels** from your dashboard with the completion video, raw footage, notes and the difficulty you suggest (up to 5 waiting at once)
- Admins review the submissions in the new Levels tab
- The **Fetch** button fills in the name, creator and in-game rating from the Geometry Dash servers, the difficulty from the Demon Ladder and the in-game thumbnail
- Moving a level can now take our difficulties along: the faces stay in place and the levels move through them. A dialog shows every change before it happens
