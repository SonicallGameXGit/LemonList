The very first version of LemonList, the demon list of our community. Unlike the classic lists, every demon counts here, **starting from Easy Demons**.

- A Geometry Dash look made from the game's own textures, buttons and fonts
- The **Main List** of verified levels and the **Future List** of levels waiting to be verified
- Level pages with the preview, video, verifier, records and both difficulties: the in-game rating and our own
- The **Players** leaderboard with points, ranks and the hardest level of everyone
- Accounts: register, log in with your email or username, verify your email, reset your password, change your email or delete your account
