---
title: Better lists
date: 2026-10-23
---

Long lists are easier to browse now, and it's clearer how to get your own level on the list.

- Lists are split into **pages** of 25, with arrows and page numbers at the bottom
- Beat a level that isn't on the list? The new **Request level** button above the Main List and the Future List takes you right there
- Level cells show who verified the level next to its creator
- Engagement got a star and List points a diamond, so ratings don't look like likes anymore
- Your submissions on the dashboard look like the Main List, with the level's face, place and preview, 5 per page
