LemonList now tells you what's new. Every update since the very first version is written down, so you can catch up on everything that changed and see the latest news first.

- The new **Changelogs** page lists every update, the newest first
- Big updates keep their smaller follow-ups inside, press **Show updates** to open them
- The newest updates are right on the home page
- Every page links to the changelogs at the bottom
