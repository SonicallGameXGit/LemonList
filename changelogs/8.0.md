title: Skillsets
date: 2026-10-24
---

Now you can see what each level is really about and what every player is best at, all decided by the community.

- Beat a Main List level? Pick any **skillsets** you think it needs on its page and send your vote
- Every level shows its skillsets, with the most voted one scoring the highest
- Players get a **main skillset** from the levels they beat, shown as a tag next to their name on the leaderboard, level records, latest completions and their profile
- Profiles show the whole set of a player's skillsets
- Don't want it? Hide your tag or your profile block in **Settings**
